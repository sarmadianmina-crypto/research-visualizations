U.S. Military Expenditure and Real Oil Prices During Major Geopolitical Energy Conflicts (1970–2026)

Real oil price expressed in constant 1982–84 U.S. dollars
Method: Real Oil Price = Nominal Oil Price × (100/CPI), with CPI indexed to 1982–84 = 100. Shaded areas identify selected geopolitical energy-conflict periods. The 2024–2026 Hormuz period is displayed as a scenario, consistent with the uploaded dataset rather than as an observed historical conflict interval.
Source: Sarmadian (2026), Geopolitical Energy Dataset; author’s compilation, calculations, analysis, and visualization.
